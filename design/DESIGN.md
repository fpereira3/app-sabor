## Brand & Style

The design system embodies an artisanal culinary editorial experience, bridging high-end gastronomic curation with immediate geospatial utility. It targets discerning diners, food explorers, and epicurean travelers who view dining out as a cultural journey rather than merely a transaction.

The aesthetic philosophy draws on contemporary editorial minimalism grounded in tactile warmth. It rejects the sterile, clinical appearance of generic mapping software in favor of sun-baked terracotta, warm linen backdrops, and deliberate typographic rhythm. The interface evokes the feel of a beautifully typeset food journal laid over an intuitive, fluid map. Visual touchpoints are refined, composed, and sensory—inviting exploration through warm ambient glows, generous negative space, crisp hairline dividers, and deliberate micro-interactions.

## Colors

The palette balances intense artisanal warmth with crisp digital utility:

- **Primary (`#EA580C`)**: An earthy, toasted terracotta red-orange that provides culinary energy, focal anchoring, and active state emphasis. Used selectively for primary call-to-actions, active map pins, and signature editorial highlights.
- **Secondary (`#10B981`)**: An organic emerald green representing real-time availability, table openings, open status, and positive culinary validation.
- **Tertiary (`#F59E0B`)**: An amber glaze denoting limited availability, scarce seating, reservations nearing capacity, or curated seasonal specials.
- **Status / Critical (`#EF4444`)**: A coral crimson strictly reserved for fully booked slots, closed venues, and destructive actions.
- **Neutrals & Surfaces**: Built from a warm stone progression rather than cold industrial grays:
  - Canvas / Map Underlay: `#FAF9F6` (Alabaster cream)
  - Surface Raised / Cards: `#FFFFFF` (Pure white)
  - Surface Muted / Chips: `#F5F3EF` (Tuscan wash)
  - Border Subdued: `#E7E5E4` (Stone 200)
  - Text Primary: `#1C1917` (Deep roasted charcoal)
  - Text Secondary: `#78716C` (Warm slate)
  - Text Tertiary: `#A8A29E` (Stone 400)

## Typography

Typography establishes an editorial atmosphere through a structured pairing:

- **Epilogue (Headlines & Editorial Callouts)**: A distinctive, geometric sans-serif with architectural presence and humanist warmth. It delivers punchy, confident restaurant names, neighborhood titles, and curated badges without feeling cold or overly technical.
- **Manrope (Body, Metadata, & Controls)**: A modern, open neo-grotesque font offering legibility at dense scales. Its clean geometric curves guarantee effortless scanning of addresses, menu descriptions, opening hours, and micro-labels.

All headings prioritize high-contrast hierarchy with tight negative letter tracking to reinforce editorial polish. Captions and metadata rely on medium to semi-bold weights of Manrope with slight positive tracking for crisp readability on mobile screens against map tiles.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout architecture relies on a full-bleed geospatial viewport overlaid with dynamic floating surfaces and bottom sheets:

- **Mobile (< 768px)**: A 4-column contextual canvas. Edge margins are fixed at `1rem` (16px) to maximize interactive mapping surface. Sheet modals snap between peek (120px), preview (340px), and full-bleed editorial view (100dvh).
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px)**: An 8-column layout. The map remains visible on the right 60% while a permanent floating scroll rail of 40% width docks on the left, using `1.5rem` gutters.
- **Desktop (> 1024px)**: A 12-column split viewport. The interactive map controls the background canvas, flanked by an editorial side-panel spanning 4 columns (fixed max-width 440px) with `2rem` gutters and `2.5rem` outer margins.

The spacing rhythm is strictly based on a 4px/8px incremental scale, providing consistent vertical cadences between culinary metadata, tags, and imagery.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses warm ambient lighting that simulates physical paper and frosted materials suspended over daylight cartography:

- **Base Level (Map Canvas)**: `0px` elevation, completely flat.
- **Elevation 1 (Floating Chips & Utility Buttons)**: `0 2px 8px -1px rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.08), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.04)`. Outlined with a `1px` stroke of `#E7E5E4` (Stone 200).
- **Elevation 2 (Preview Cards & Search Shells)**: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.12), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.06)`. Surface fill utilizes `#FFFFFF` with a subtle warm undertone.
- **Elevation 3 (Expanded Bottom Sheets & Modals)**: `0 20px 40px -8px rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.18), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.08)`.
- **Blur & Glass Accents**: Translucent utility bars and search shells implement `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` over a `rgba(250, 249, 246, 0.88)` wash, maintaining map visibility while ensuring high typographic contrast.

## Shapes

The design system employs a `level 2` roundedness philosophy, pairing structured geometry with comfortable ergonomics:

- **Base Radius (`rounded-md`, 0.5rem / 8px)**: Standard inputs, actionable menu rows, mini status tags, and utility buttons.
- **Prominent Radius (`rounded-lg`, 1rem / 16px)**: Floating preview cards, culinary imagery containers, category pill filters, and dialogs.
- **Hero Sheet Radius (`rounded-xl`, 1.5rem / 24px)**: Top handles of pullable bottom sheets, sticky contextual summary banners, and modal overlays.
- **Full Pill (`rounded-full`)**: Search bars, quick filter chips, map pin badges, and directional icon toggles.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid terracotta background (`#EA580C`), crisp white text (`#FFFFFF`), `rounded-full` or `rounded-lg` (16px), height of 48px on mobile for generous touch affordance. Active states apply a warm darker shade (`#C2410C`).
- **Secondary**: Stone tint surface (`#F5F3EF`), text `#1C1917`, zero shadow, soft hairline border (`#E7E5E4`).
- **Floating Map Action (Fab)**: Pure white background, `rounded-full`, 44x44px or 48x48px, elevated with Elevation 1 and crisp icon centering.

### Chips & Filter Pills
- Compact (36px height), `rounded-full`, padding `0.5rem 1rem`.
- Inactive: `#FFFFFF` fill with `1px solid #E7E5E4`, text `#78716C`.
- Active: Terracotta tint background (`#FFF7ED`), border `1.5px solid #EA580C`, text `#EA580C` with a leading check or count dot.

### Restaurant Cards (Floating & Sheet)
- Pure white surface with `rounded-lg` (16px) corners and Elevation 2.
- 16:10 or 1:1 aspect ratio photography featuring subtle warm vignette.
- Top right overlays featuring availability badges (`#10B981` Emerald for open/available, `#F59E0B` Amber for last tables).
- Structured Epilogue headline for title, followed by price, cuisine genre, and distance in Manrope body-sm.

### Form Inputs & Search Bar
- Omnipresent top floating search bar styled with `rounded-full`, height 52px, frosted surface (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.92)`), `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`.
- Left-aligned terracotta discovery glyph, right-aligned filter trigger button.
- Clean text input placeholder in `#78716C`, focused ring uses `#EA580C` with 2px soft outer glow (`rgba(234, 88, 12, 0.2)`).

### Map Markers & Spot Indicators
- Custom teardrop markers with circular photographic or category icons.
- Default state: `#FFFFFF` background with terracotta contour border and drop shadow.
- Selected state: Scales 1.25x with full `#EA580C` fill, white glyph, and a floating price/rating tag above the marker pin.

### Lists & Menu Items
- Separated by hairline dividers (`1px solid #F5F3EF`).
- Dish listings utilize clean flex rows: title and editorial description on the left, price and dietary badges on the right.
